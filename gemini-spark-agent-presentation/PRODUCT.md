# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is a mixed group of Gemini Spark product managers, designers, engineers, and leaders watching a 14–15 minute keynote through Google Meet. They need to understand a product opportunity, trust the evidence behind it, and see Barron as someone who already reasons about personal agents with product-level sophistication.

## Product Purpose

This HTML keynote uses Barron’s completed condo-insurance operation with Alfred to show that ordinary Gemini conversations can reveal latent delegable work. It argues for Gemini as the discovery surface and Spark as the context-preserving execution layer, while respecting the permissions, persistence, auditability, recovery, and approval infrastructure required for consequential action.

Success means the audience remembers one idea: users ask for help, not agents. The presentation should make the transition from ordinary conversation to supervised execution feel obvious only after the audience has experienced the story.

## Brand Personality

Intimate, exact, accountable.

The presentation should feel personally lived rather than marketed, technically credible rather than magical, and emotionally warm without becoming sentimental. Barron’s voice is confident, concise, and product-minded.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI launch deck, SaaS metrics presentation, speculative product mockup, developer terminal demo, or prestige-tech advertisement. Avoid Gemini rainbow gradients, glowing neural networks, glassmorphism, beige editorial minimalism, identical card grids, fake dashboards, device-mockup repetition, mascot theatrics, and oversized dollar figures detached from evidence. Do not repeat the retro mission-patch or NASA aesthetic used in earlier decks.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the argument through continuity.** The original question, its context, permissions, work, and evidence should remain visibly connected.
2. **Let authentic artifacts carry authority.** Screenshots, documents, messages, and execution records are evidence, not decoration.
3. **Preserve the reveal.** The audience should feel the job expand over 28 days before seeing the financial outcome.
4. **Make trust structural.** Approval gates, bounded authority, persistence, recovery, and auditability must be visible in the visual grammar.
5. **Return to the human.** The close is about time and attention returned, not agent spectacle or financial victory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target strong WCAG contrast and Google Meet legibility at 1920×1080, 1366×768, 1280×720, and short desktop heights. Use large re-typeset transcriptions beside authentic crops, never rely on color alone, keep important lines at least 3 px after scaling, provide keyboard and touch navigation, visible focus, meaningful alt text, deterministic direct links, print-safe final states, and a complete reduced-motion choreography. Mobile should become a deliberate vertical reading order rather than a squeezed desktop deck.
