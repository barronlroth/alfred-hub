# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Barron and Nina, reviewing luxury minimoon options together on a desktop after their October 3, 2026 wedding in Miami. They are exploring rather than booking, and need enough atmosphere and practical truth to reach a confident shortlist.

## Product Purpose

Create an immersive decision surface for choosing a five-to-seven-night minimoon beginning October 5 or 6, 2026. The site should make each destination feel visitable through real hotel imagery while keeping price, weather, travel friction, romance, service, activities, and drawbacks easy to compare.

## Brand Personality

Transporting, discerning, candid. Luxurious without acting impressed by mere expense. Warm enough to feel personal, rigorous enough to prevent a beautiful mistake.

## Anti-references

Not a booking-engine grid, generic travel blog, affiliate listicle, beige luxury template, glossy brochure that hides drawbacks, or a SaaS dashboard wearing resort photography. No fake precision, broken remote imagery, interchangeable cards, or equal praise for every option.

## Design Principles

1. Make the place felt before making it scored.
2. Separate observed pricing from estimates and label confidence plainly.
3. Give every option a role, a reason to choose it, and an honest catch.
4. Let imagery carry atmosphere while typography carries judgment.
5. Help two people converge rather than endlessly browse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, semantic landmarks and headings, descriptive image alt text, WCAG AA contrast for functional text, and reduced-motion support. Desktop is primary, but the page must remain usable on smaller screens.
