Field notes

What the numbers mean.

Rates

Monday figures are USD planning ranges for six nights. Tuesday totals are plainly labeled proportional five-night estimates derived from that research, not live quotes. “Exact date” means a direct or third-party October 5–11 signal was captured. “Adjacent date” uses nearby inventory. “Generic signal” uses published or current public rates. Taxes, fees, parking, and worthwhile room categories are included where research supported them.

Rank

The top ten balance romance, privacy, service, food and spa, activities, weather, routing, and budget. The order is editorial judgment for this specific trip. It is not a universal hotel score.

Weather and routes

Weather describes early October patterns and material disruption risks. Route notes consider the full MIA → stay → SFO journey, not only the easiest flight segment.

Images and words

All 75 images are saved locally and were audited for integrity, resolution, duplication, and relevance. Captions, source names, and original source links are attached to each frame. Quotes retain their source link.
