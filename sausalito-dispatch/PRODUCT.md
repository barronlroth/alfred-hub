# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Barron, Nina, and four friends coordinating a split arrival in Sausalito from their phones while already in motion.

## Product Purpose

Turn one awkward day-of handoff into a shared, glanceable plan. The tool calculates when the Dunphy Park group should start walking, keeps the restaurant and route actions one tap away, and carries the group through the post-lunch walk without a group-chat archaeology dig.

## Brand Personality

Playful, decisive, sunlit. It should feel like a collectible day ticket with Alfred quietly running dispatch, not a logistics dashboard.

## Anti-references

No generic travel cards, SaaS dashboards, faux glass panels, dense itinerary spreadsheets, or decorative mobile-device chrome. Do not make six adults operate project-management software to eat lunch.

## Design Principles

1. One live decision owns the screen: when the park crew should walk.
2. Geography must read faster than prose.
3. Every external action is one tap and names its destination.
4. Personality comes from the ticket object, not ornamental clutter.
5. The interface remains useful if the quoted wait changes three times.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve browser zoom, use 44px minimum touch targets, visible focus states, semantic controls, text alternatives for route graphics, and reduced-motion support.
